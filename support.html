Firefly Support

Help and answers for parents and guardians

Firefly is the play app that knows when to stop. Every session ends with a goodnight rather than a cutoff — no ads, no tracking, and nothing sold to your child. If something isn't working the way you expect, the answers below cover the questions we hear most often.

Everyday questions

How does the daily play time work?

Firefly gives your child 60 minutes of play each day by default. That default is the same for every family, with or without Firefly Plus, and it isn't a setting you change — on days when you'd like more, you can add time from the parent area (see the next question). The clock only counts time actually spent in the app with the screen in front of them — if your child switches away, puts the device down with Firefly in the background, or you take the device back, the time pauses and picks up again when they return.

The day's time resets on its own at midnight: any time you added is cleared, and the next day starts again at the 60-minute default. Nothing carries over, and nothing needs to be set up or reset by hand.

Can I give my child a little more time today?

Yes. In the parent area you'll find "Add 15 minutes today". You can tap it as many times as you need to, and any extra time you add applies only to the current day — it clears with everything else at midnight, so a generous afternoon never quietly becomes the new normal.

What happens when the time runs out?

Firefly waits for a natural stopping point. It will never cut your child off in the middle of an activity — the current round finishes first, and only then does the app settle into the Sleep Screen: a quiet goodnight, your child's chosen avatar tucked into bed, and a single "Goodnight" button.

Nothing on that screen competes for your child's attention or sells them anything. No score, no "play again," no offer to buy anything, and no way back into the activities until the next day, unless a grown-up adds time. The one other thing there is a small, deliberately quiet "For grown-ups" link, set apart from the goodnight and meant for you rather than your child. That's the whole idea — the app ends the session so you don't have to be the one who does.

My child is stuck on the goodnight screen. Is something wrong?

No — that's working as intended. Once the day's play time is used up, the Sleep Screen stays until the next day begins. The kindest thing to do is close the app; Firefly will be ready again tomorrow. If you'd like to give a little more time instead, use "Add 15 minutes today" in the parent area (reachable from the small "For grown-ups" link on the goodnight screen).

The parent area

What is the math question I'm being asked?

That's the Parent Gate. Before Firefly will open anything meant for an adult — subscription screens, links that leave the app, or the parent area itself — it asks for the answer to a simple multiplication problem. It's there so a young child can't wander into those places on their own.

If a particular problem is awkward, tap "Give me a different one" for a new one. Nothing is penalised and there's no limit on tries.

Where do I find settings?

Everything for parents lives in one place, behind the Parent Gate. What you can change there: add 15 more minutes to today, switch Sounds on or off, switch Background music on or off, and manage your subscription. You can reach it from the small parent icon at the bottom of the app, or from the "For grown-ups" link on the goodnight screen.

The same screen also shows — but does not let you change — how much play time is left today and, for each category, how much of it is free and how much needs Plus. That listing is a status display, not a set of switches: there's no way to turn an individual category on or off, because what's available is decided entirely by whether you subscribe (plus the free activity that rotates in each day).

Can I turn the sound off?

Yes — there are two independent switches in the parent area. Sounds covers the gentle chimes and the spoken activity introductions; Background music covers the quiet loop that plays during some activities. You can turn either off without affecting the other. Firefly also respects your device's silent switch, so flipping that silences everything.

Firefly Plus

What does Firefly Plus include?

Firefly Plus unlocks every activity family — 42 today, across six categories — including the full Puzzles and tracing collections, and everything in every weekly theme as it rotates. Monthly and yearly subscriptions are available; the app shows the current price and billing period for your App Store region before you purchase. Apple can show these prices before you allow RevenueCat; choosing Agree & subscribe saves that permission and begins the purchase, which Apple then separately confirms. If a purchase is cancelled or fails, the saved permission can remain. Firefly stays ad-free and tracking-free either way — a subscription adds more to play, it never removes something we've taken away. Plus doesn't change the daily play time: every family gets the same 60-minute default.

We don't subscribe. Is there still plenty to play?

Yes. 19 whole activity families are permanently free, including all of Breathing. Tracing uppercase A–F and numbers 1–3 is also permanently free. The remaining tracing figures are included with Plus. On top of that, one Plus activity is unlocked for everyone each day — it appears on the activity list marked "Today's pick," and a different one comes around the next day. There's no countdown and no pressure attached to it; it's simply a rotating bonus. Tracing and Puzzles are excluded from that rotation.

I already paid, but Firefly isn't showing Plus. How do I restore it?

Behind the Parent Gate, tap "Restore Purchases". If purchase-data sharing is not yet allowed, the button reads "Agree & restore purchases": choosing it saves your permission for RevenueCat and then begins the restore. Passing the Parent Gate or opening the privacy details does not give permission. The saved permission applies to future connections. You can keep playing without it, and previously saved Plus access is preserved. You'll find the restore button in two places: on the subscription screen, and in the parent area under your plan details. It's always available, whether or not Firefly currently thinks you're subscribed.

Restoring uses the Apple ID that made the original purchase, so make sure the device is signed in to that account. If your subscription still doesn't appear afterwards, email us with a description of the problem. We may ask for the Firefly order number from your Apple receipt to investigate. Do not send your Apple password or payment details.

How do I turn subscription checks off?

Go to Grown-ups → Purchase privacy → Stop sharing purchase data, then choose Stop sharing and save. Wait for the confirmation that your choice is saved, then fully close Firefly from the app switcher and reopen it. New purchase-service calls from the app stop immediately, but RevenueCat may finish work already in progress or make its own background or foreground requests until that full restart. Returning Home or switching apps is not enough.

After a successful save and restart, checks stay off until you explicitly allow them again, for example by choosing Agree & subscribe or Agree & restore purchases. If saving fails, use Retry saving withdrawal before restarting; the app cannot confirm that your choice will persist until it is saved. Free play and saved Plus access remain available. A purchase or restore already started may finish. Turning checks off does not cancel an Apple subscription or delete records held by Apple or RevenueCat. Logging out of the parent area also does not withdraw subscription consent.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us — so cancelling happens in your App Store account settings, and takes effect at the end of the period you've already paid for. The quickest route is the "Cancel subscription" link in the parent area, which opens the right page in Settings for you. You can also get there manually: open Settings, tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions, and choose Firefly.

Because Apple handles this directly, we can't cancel or refund a subscription on your behalf. For a refund request, use Apple's reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy

What does Firefly know about my child?

What your child enters and how they play stays on your device. The nickname, avatar, progress, recent-play history and play time are stored locally. Firefly does not transmit them to us or RevenueCat. Device backups and secure-storage exceptions are described in the Privacy Policy. There are no accounts and no ads, Firefly doesn't use analytics to study how your child plays, and it doesn't track anyone across other companies' apps and websites.

You can view Recent play in the parent area and remove that record with "Clear play history". Entries are kept for up to 30 days, with at most 2,000 entries stored. Earlier installations may also retain old setup preferences; the current setup does not ask for an age range or learning preferences.

After a grown-up explicitly allows subscription checks, Firefly uses RevenueCat. Whether or not you subscribe, RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier, the device's identifier for vendor (not the advertising identifier), purchase and subscription information, an App Tracking Transparency status value (RevenueCat's software can send it without requesting tracking permission, and if Apple's framework is unavailable the fallback may not match the real device setting; Firefly never requests tracking permission), technical details such as the device model and iOS version, and service metadata such as when a record was created and last connected. Firefly does not request location permission or use GPS; RevenueCat may derive a country from the IP address. RevenueCat's dashboard shows us purchases and subscriptions — never child nicknames, activity choices, progress or play duration. Our full Privacy Policy lists exactly what is shared and why.

How do I make a privacy request?

Email agency.purpleparrot@gmail.com with the subject “Firefly privacy request”. Tell us what you would like reviewed or deleted. Do not include your child's name or play history. Your email address alone does not identify an anonymous RevenueCat record; we may ask for relevant purchase information and explain any lookup limitations. You do not need to purchase or turn subscription checks on to ask for help. See the Privacy Policy for the data and deletion details.
