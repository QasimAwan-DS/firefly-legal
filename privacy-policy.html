Privacy Policy for Firefly

Last updated: November 8, 2026

In short: Firefly has no accounts and no ads, and it does not track your child across other companies' apps or websites. What your child enters and how they play — their nickname, avatar, progress, play time and a short record of recent play — is kept locally. Firefly does not send it to us or RevenueCat. Device backups are explained in Section 4. Only after a grown-up explicitly allows subscription checks, Firefly uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous identifier, a device identifier, purchase and subscription information, and some technical information from the device. Once allowed, these checks can run on future launches, whether or not you subscribe. Section 5 lists exactly what, and why.

1. Who we are

Firefly is a calm, low-stimulation play app for children, developed and published by Qasim Farooq ("we," "us," or "our"). This policy explains what information Firefly collects, how it is used, and the choices available to you as a parent or guardian.

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at agency.purpleparrot@gmail.com.

2. Our commitment to children's privacy

Firefly is designed for children. We built it so that Firefly does not send what your child types, chooses or plays to us or RevenueCat. We do not ask for your child's full name, birthday, contact details, photos or location. There are:

  • No account creation, login, or sign-up of any kind
  • No email address, postal address, phone number, or other contact information collected from a child
  • No photos, camera, or microphone access
  • No chat, messaging, or any way for a child to communicate with anyone through the app
  • No user-generated content shared publicly or with other users
  • No social features of any kind

During first-time setup, Firefly asks for a first name or nickname so it can greet your child inside the app. You can skip this step entirely — there is a "Skip for now" button, nothing is required, and if no name is entered Firefly simply says "friend" instead. If you do enter one, it is stored only on your own device. It is never transmitted to us, never linked to any identifier, and never shared with anyone. See Section 4.

The one thing that does leave the device is the subscription check described in Section 5. It sends identifiers, purchase information, and technical information about the device — not anything your child enters. Section 5 lists each item.

3. What Firefly does not do

Firefly shows no ads and does not use analytics to study how your child plays. Specifically, we do not:

  • Ask for tracking permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt, or track your device across apps or websites (RevenueCat's software can send an ATT status value without requesting permission — see Section 5)
  • Use Apple's AdSupport framework or collect any advertising identifier (IDFA)
  • Add analytics software to the app, or send any record of which activities your child plays, their progress, their play time, or anything they enter off the device. (RevenueCat's purchase dashboard, described in Section 5, contains purchase and subscription records and service metadata, such as when an anonymous record was created and when it last connected. It contains no child nicknames, activity choices, progress or play-duration records.)
  • Show advertisements of any kind
  • Sell, rent, or share any data with data brokers or advertisers
  • Request device location permission or use GPS. RevenueCat may derive a country from an IP address, as explained in Section 5.

4. Information that stays on your device

Firefly keeps the following on your device, using standard iOS storage. Firefly does not transmit these records to us or RevenueCat. Device backups are described below:

  • The first name or nickname entered during setup, used only to greet your child inside the app
  • On installations that used an earlier setup flow, any previously saved age range and learning preferences. The current setup no longer asks these questions.
  • Which avatar your child selected during setup (a preset illustration, not a photo)
  • How much play time has been used today, and any extra time a parent has added — both reset automatically each day
  • Progress within the app's activities, used only to gently adjust difficulty on your own device
  • Sound and music preferences
  • Your versioned choice to allow subscription checks, paired with a device-only secure record. These local records are not sent to RevenueCat and are separate from the temporary Parent Gate pass.
  • A short record of recent play, shown to grown-ups as "Recent play": which activities were opened and on which day and time, how long each activity screen was open, how many rounds were completed or pictures and breathing sessions finished, the name of a tracing figure, Shape Builder picture or story where the activity has one, whether a hint was shown (in the three activities that show one after a wrong choice), and the difficulty step in use. It never includes your child's name, touches, drawings or voice. Each entry is kept for up to 30 days (and no more than 2,000 entries in total), then removed automatically.

You can clear the recent-play record at any time: open For grown-ups, then Recent play, then "Clear play history". Clearing it removes only that record; play time, setup choices, difficulty settings and the subscription stay as they are.

Firefly also keeps a copy of your subscription status (whether Firefly Plus is active) so that Plus stays unlocked if the internet is unavailable. That status comes from RevenueCat (Section 5); the copy on your device is not sent anywhere.

We have no way to see any of this information, and no server that stores it. Deleting the app removes it from your device. (iOS may keep the saved subscription status — a yes-or-no value in the device's secure storage — and the device-only consent record after the app is deleted. The secure record alone does not authorize checks: the matching app record is also required.) Like other app data, this information may be included in backups of your device that you or your device make, such as iCloud or computer backups; those backups are controlled by you and your backup provider, and we cannot access them.

5. Subscriptions and RevenueCat

If you choose to subscribe to Firefly Plus, payment is handled entirely through Apple's In-App Purchase system. We do not collect or see your payment details — Apple processes all payments directly.

Firefly uses RevenueCat, a subscription-management service that acts as our service provider, to manage purchases and subscriptions. Before initializing RevenueCat, Firefly asks a grown-up behind the Parent Gate to explicitly allow subscription checks. Apple can show subscription prices before that permission is given. Choosing Agree & subscribe or Agree & restore purchases saves the permission and then begins the chosen action; Apple separately confirms any purchase. Passing the Parent Gate, viewing plans or opening the privacy details does not give permission. Without a matching current permission record, Firefly uses only the previously saved entitlement on the device; free play and previously saved Plus access remain available.

The saved permission applies to future connections, including during child play, and can remain saved if a purchase is cancelled or fails. Existing installations are not assumed to have consented. A missing, older or unreadable consent record requires a new choice. Once checks are allowed, RevenueCat receives:

  • An anonymous identifier that RevenueCat's software creates for this installation of Firefly. It is not tied to a name or email, but it stays the same so that a purchase can be recognized again.
  • The device's identifier for vendor — an identifier Apple gives each developer's apps on a device. It is not the advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • Purchase and subscription information: which Firefly Plus plan was bought, when, and whether it is active or renewing, along with Apple's transaction records
  • An App Tracking Transparency status value. RevenueCat's software can send this value without requesting tracking permission. If Apple's framework is unavailable, RevenueCat's fallback may not reflect the actual device setting. Firefly does not request tracking permission.
  • Basic technical information: the device model, iOS version, Firefly's version, the device's language settings and App Store country, and, as with any internet service, the device's IP address
  • Purchase-related records and service metadata that RevenueCat creates, such as when a record was created and when the app last connected

Firefly does not send RevenueCat your name or email, or anything your child enters or plays, including nicknames, activity choices, progress or play duration.

RevenueCat uses this information to make purchases work: verifying receipts with Apple, preventing fraud, restoring purchases, and unlocking Firefly Plus. It keeps purchase records under the anonymous identifier, so those records are connected to that identifier and this device's purchases even though they carry no name.

RevenueCat also gives us a purchase dashboard. It shows purchase and subscription records and totals — for example, how many subscriptions started, renewed, or were cancelled. RevenueCat describes this use as analytics of purchase information. The dashboard contains nothing about which activities your child plays, how long they play, or how they are doing, because Firefly never sends that information.

Firefly does not request tracking permission, does not enable Apple Search Ads attribution collection, and does not connect RevenueCat to any advertising or analytics service. Firefly never adds an email address, alias, or any child-derived information to what RevenueCat receives. You can read RevenueCat's own privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Firefly does not request location permission and does not use GPS. RevenueCat's documentation says its customer profile may use a country derived from the last-seen IP address when a transaction country is unavailable. It states that the IP address is dropped after determining the country. This is separate from using GPS or requesting device location permission. See RevenueCat's customer-profile documentation.

Turning subscription checks off

Go to Grown-ups → Purchase privacy → Stop sharing purchase data, then choose Stop sharing and save. Wait for the confirmation that your choice is saved, then fully close Firefly from the app switcher and reopen it. New purchase-service calls from the app stop immediately, but RevenueCat may finish work already in progress or make its own background or foreground requests until that full restart. Returning Home or switching apps is not enough.

After a successful save and restart, checks stay off until you explicitly allow them again, for example by choosing Agree & subscribe or Agree & restore purchases. If saving fails, use Retry saving withdrawal before restarting; the app cannot confirm that your choice will persist until it is saved. Free play and saved Plus access remain available. A purchase or restore already started may finish. Turning checks off does not cancel an Apple subscription or delete records held by Apple or RevenueCat. Logging out of the parent area also does not withdraw subscription consent.

6. Parental controls

Firefly includes a Parent Gate — a simple math challenge designed so a young child cannot access purchase screens, external links, or settings on their own. Everything behind the Parent Gate, including subscription management and the option to restore a previous purchase, is intended for a parent or guardian.

7. Children under 13 and parental rights

Firefly does not ask for, and does not send, anything your child enters. The information described in Section 4 is stored locally, subject to the backup and secure-storage exceptions explained there; the recent-play record can also be cleared at any time from For grown-ups. The subscription records described in Section 5 are held by RevenueCat under an anonymous identifier rather than a name.

If you would like to ask about those records or have us request their deletion, if you believe your child has somehow provided us with personal information, or if you have any question about your rights under COPPA, the GDPR, or another applicable law, please contact us at agency.purpleparrot@gmail.com and we will explain what information is needed to handle your request. Please do not send your child’s name or play history. Cancellation of an Apple subscription is separate from a request to delete records.

Retention and deletion

Firefly does not automatically delete RevenueCat records when you turn off subscription checks, uninstall the app or cancel a subscription. Those actions are separate from a deletion request. RevenueCat's published Data Processing Addendum describes processing during the service agreement and return or destruction on the developer's request at the end of that agreement, subject to backup and archival practices. We do not promise that uninstalling Firefly removes those records or that every backup is erased immediately.

You can ask us to review or request deletion of records by emailing the address below. Because Firefly has no account or email sign-in, your email address alone does not identify a RevenueCat record. For a purchase-related request, we may ask for the Firefly order number from your Apple receipt and a redacted receipt showing the relevant purchase. Do not send your Apple password, payment details, government identification or unrelated purchases. We will check the available record and your connection to it before taking action. If we cannot identify a matching record, we will explain that limitation and the available next step. You do not need to buy anything or allow subscription checks again to contact us.

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use that correspondence to answer your request. Please avoid including information about your child.

8. Data security

Because the information described in Section 4 stays on your device and is not transmitted to us, its security is governed by your device's own protections (such as your device passcode). We do not operate servers that store personal information about Firefly's users. RevenueCat stores the subscription information described in Section 5 on its own systems as our service provider.

9. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where required, provide notice within the app. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

10. Contact us

If you have questions, concerns, or feedback about this policy or Firefly's privacy practices, please contact us at agency.purpleparrot@gmail.com.
