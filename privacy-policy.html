Privacy Policy for Firefly

Last updated: July 24, 2026

In short: Firefly does not collect, store, or share any personal information about your child. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and nothing about your child ever leaves the device.

1. Who we are

Firefly is a calm, low-stimulation play app for children, developed and published by Qasim Farooq ("we," "us," or "our"). This policy explains what information Firefly collects, how it is used, and the choices available to you as a parent or guardian.

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at agency.purpleparrot@gmail.com.

2. Our commitment to children's privacy

Firefly is designed for children and is offered in Apple's Kids Category. We built Firefly around a simple rule: we do not collect personal information from children, and nothing your child enters is ever sent to us. There are:

  • No account creation, login, or sign-up of any kind
  • No email address, postal address, phone number, or other contact information collected from a child
  • No photos, camera, or microphone access
  • No chat, messaging, or any way for a child to communicate with anyone through the app
  • No user-generated content shared publicly or with other users
  • No social features of any kind

During first-time setup, Firefly asks for a first name or nickname so it can greet your child inside the app. You can skip this step entirely — there is a "Skip for now" button, nothing is required, and if no name is entered Firefly simply says "friend" instead. If you do enter one, it is stored only on your own device. It is never transmitted to us, never linked to any identifier, and never shared with anyone. See Section 4.

This policy is intended to comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) including provisions relevant to children's data, and Apple's App Store guidelines for apps in the Kids Category.

3. Information we do not collect

Firefly does not use advertising or analytics software of any kind. Specifically, we do not:

  • Use Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework or track your device across apps or websites
  • Link or use Apple's AdSupport framework or any advertising identifier (IDFA)
  • Use any third-party analytics software
  • Show advertisements of any kind
  • Sell, rent, or share any data with data brokers or advertisers
  • Collect location data

4. Information stored on your device

Firefly stores a small amount of information locally on your device, using standard iOS storage. This information never leaves your device except where noted in Section 5 below, and is not linked to your identity in any way we can access.

  • The first name or nickname entered during setup, used only to greet your child inside the app
  • The age range selected during setup, and the setup preferences chosen at that time (what you'd like Firefly to help with, and which areas to focus on)
  • Which avatar your child selected during setup (a preset illustration, not a photo)
  • How much play time has been used today, and any extra time a parent has added — both reset automatically each day
  • Progress within the app's activities, used only to gently adjust difficulty on your own device
  • Sound and music preferences
  • Subscription status (whether Firefly Plus is active), used to unlock content

None of this information is transmitted to us. We have no way to see it, and no server that stores it. Deleting the app removes all of it from your device.

5. Subscriptions and payment processing

If you choose to subscribe to Firefly Plus, purchases are handled entirely through Apple's In-App Purchase system. We do not collect or see your payment details — Apple processes all payments directly.

To verify that a subscription is active, Firefly uses RevenueCat, a subscription-management service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous, randomly generated identifier associated with your purchase — never your name, email, or any information that identifies your child. RevenueCat's role is limited to confirming your subscription is valid so the app can unlock the content you've paid for. You can read RevenueCat's own privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

No customer email, alias, or any child-derived information is ever set on this identifier.

6. Parental controls

Firefly includes a Parent Gate — a simple math challenge designed so a young child cannot access purchase screens, external links, or settings on their own. Everything behind the Parent Gate, including subscription management and the option to restore a previous purchase, is intended for a parent or guardian.

7. Children under 13 and parental rights

Because Firefly does not collect personal information from children, there is generally no child data for a parent to request, correct, or delete. The information described in Section 4 stays on your device and is removed entirely when you delete the app. If you believe your child has somehow provided us with personal information, or if you have any question about your rights under COPPA, GDPR, or another applicable law, please contact us at agency.purpleparrot@gmail.com and we will investigate and respond promptly.

8. Data security

Because the information described in Section 4 stays on your device and is not transmitted to us, its security is governed by your device's own protections (such as your device passcode). We do not operate servers that store personal information about Firefly's users.

9. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where required, provide notice within the app. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

10. Contact us

If you have questions, concerns, or feedback about this policy or Firefly's privacy practices, please contact us at agency.purpleparrot@gmail.com.
